For our general privacy policy covering the Reviewcap app and this website, see reviewcap.ai/privacy.

Why you received this email

We identified your store as a merchant that already uses a product review app (Judge.me or Loox) and has enough reviews to benefit from AI-generated review summaries, which is what Reviewcap offers. We contact you once to introduce it. We will not contact you again unless you reply.

What data we hold about you

  • Your store name, domain and country.
  • The business contact email address we used to reach you, the page it came from, and the date we found it.
  • A short summary we generated from your store's own publicly visible product reviews, to show you an example of what Reviewcap produces.

We hold no data about your customers. We did not access anything private or account-protected. Everything we used was publicly visible on your storefront.

Where the data came from

From your store's public website (contact page, footer or imprint) and from the public review pages of the Shopify App Store. We did not buy it from a data broker or list provider.

Legal basis

We process your business contact details on the basis of our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: a single, relevant business-to-business message about a tool related to a review app you already use. We have weighed this interest against your rights in a written Legitimate Interest Assessment, which we will send you on request.

How long we keep it

  • If you do not respond, we delete your contact details, the summary and the opt-out link 180 days after the email.
  • If we never found a working contact address for your store, the data we hold is deleted after 90 days.
  • If you opt out, your contact details are deleted immediately.

In every case we keep a minimal record of your store name, domain and country, so that we do not contact you again and do not process your store a second time.

Your rights

At any time and free of charge you can:

  • Object to this processing (Art. 21 GDPR). We will stop and delete your data.
  • Access the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted (Art. 15–17).
  • Ask us to restrict the processing or to give you your data in a portable format (Art. 18 and 20).
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), which is competent for Bolzano.

To use any of these, reply to the email or write to patrick@tryreviewcap.com. We act within 30 days. The fastest way to stop all contact is the do-not-contact link at the bottom of our email.

Automated decisions

None. A person selected your store and sent the email. We use no profiling and make no decision with legal effect.
